The best family organizer apps of 2026, honestly compared.

Cozi, FamilyWall, TimeTree, Skylight, the calendars you already have, and FamSync. What each one is best at, what it costs, and who should pick something else.

Read this first.

I make FamSync, one of the apps on this list. That's a conflict of interest and you should weigh what I say with it in mind. I've tried to be fair: every app here is good at something, and for plenty of families FamSync isn't the right pick. Prices and ratings are from each app's US App Store listing or website on October 20, 2026. They change, so check before you pay.

How I compared them

A family organizer only works if everyone opens it. So beyond features, I looked at five practical questions.

  1. Does it work on everyone's phone? One Android user can rule an app out.
  2. What does the free version really include? Including ads and limits.
  3. What does the paid plan cost a whole family? Some charge per person.
  4. Does it go beyond the calendar? Chores, groceries, meals.
  5. Will a teenager or a grandparent actually use it?

Cozi

Best for mixed iPhone and Android families who want the classic
Good at
Simple shared calendar, shopping and to-do lists, and a recipe box, on iPhone, Android and the web. A long track record, with about 401,000 ratings on the US App Store at 4.8 stars.
Watch out
The free version has ads. Its App Store listing names adding, editing and viewing events more than 30 days ahead as a Gold feature.
Price
Free with ads. Cozi Gold is listed at $39 in the App Store and on cozi.com; Cozi Max at $79.99.

FamilyWall

Best for families who want lots of tools in one app
Good at
A free shared calendar, shopping and task lists, recipes, family messaging and a photo gallery. Premium adds a meal planner, a budget, document storage, Google and Outlook calendar sync and a locator with arrive and leave alerts. 4.8 stars from about 24,000 ratings.
Watch out
The best parts (meal planner, location, calendar sync) are Premium, and there's a lot of app to learn.
Price
Free, or Premium at $7.99 a month or $44.99 a year, after a 30-day trial.

TimeTree

Best for a shared calendar and nothing else
Good at
Sharing calendars with several groups at once: family, a team, a book club. Chat on each event, memos, photo albums and a web version. 4.9 stars from about 93,000 ratings.
Watch out
The free version shows ads. It's a calendar, so chores, groceries and meals live elsewhere.
Price
Free with ads, or Premium at $4.49 a month or $44.99 a year.

Skylight app

Best if you have, or want, a wall calendar display
Good at
Running a Skylight Calendar on the kitchen wall, with chores and routines everyone can see from across the room. Great for kids too young for phones. 4.8 stars from about 52,000 ratings.
Watch out
It's built around Skylight's hardware, and recipe books and meal plans are a paid Plus feature.
Price
Calendar Plus is listed at $7.99 a month or $79 a year, on top of the display itself.

Google Calendar or Apple's Family calendar

Best free option with no new app
Good at
Costing nothing and needing nothing new. A Google family group comes with a shared Family calendar, and Apple Family Sharing creates a shared Family calendar in iCloud. Both cover up to six people.
Watch out
No chores, no allowance, no meal planning, and it's easy for kids' events to get lost among work meetings. Everyone needs an account, so little kids and some grandparents are left out.
Price
Free.

FamSync

The one I make
Good at
Everything in one calm, ad-free app: a shared calendar with a color per person, chores with allowance and parent OK, a grocery list that sorts by aisle, and phone-free profiles for little kids and grandparents, all free for up to four people. Premium adds a meal planner that plans around each kid's likes, dislikes and allergies, reminders that ring even on silent, Apple and Google Calendar sync, photo scanning of paper schedules, and a live family map with drive summaries (route, distance, top speed, hard braking and phone pickups). Teen drivers can see their own drives are logged, so nothing is hidden from them.
Watch out
iPhone and iPad only, no web version, and it's new: trusted by 100+ families so far, but only a handful of App Store ratings, and one developer. Repeating events, calendar sync and the meal planner are Premium.
Price
Free for up to 4 people. Premium is $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year for up to 8, with a free trial for eligible new subscribers.

Two specialist picks

For co-parents who need court-ready records

If your co-parenting plan needs message logs or records a court will accept, use an app built for that. OurFamilyWizard lists its Essentials plan at $149.99 a year per parent. TalkingParents starts at $7 a month. None of the general organizers above, FamSync included, are designed for that job. I wrote more on this in a shared calendar for co-parents.

For dinner on its own

If all you need is a meal planner, a dedicated recipe app may suit you better than a family organizer. If the hard part is picky kids, here's how FamSync plans around them.

So which one should you choose?

  • Someone uses Android: Cozi, FamilyWall or TimeTree.
  • You just want a shared calendar for free: Google's or Apple's Family calendar, or TimeTree if you don't mind ads.
  • You want a screen on the kitchen wall: Skylight.
  • Lots of features and you'll pay for them: FamilyWall.
  • All-iPhone family that wants calendar, chores, groceries and dinner in one ad-free app: try FamSync. If it's not for you, coming from Cozi or anywhere else, the free plan costs you nothing to find out.

Quick answers

What is the best family organizer app in 2026?

It depends on your family. Cozi, FamilyWall and TimeTree work across iPhone and Android. Skylight suits families with a wall display. Google's and Apple's Family calendars are free. FamSync suits iPhone families who want a calendar, chores, groceries and meal planning in one ad-free app.

Which family organizer app has no ads?

FamSync has no ads in its free or paid plan. Google's and Apple's Family calendars have none either. Cozi and TimeTree show ads in their free versions and remove them with a paid plan.

What is the best free family calendar app?

For a calendar only, Google's or Apple's built-in Family calendar is free with no ads, for up to six people. If you also want chores and a shared grocery list, FamSync is free for families of up to 4 people with no ads.

What's the best family organizer for Android and iPhone together?

Cozi, FamilyWall and TimeTree all work on both. FamSync is iPhone and iPad only for now.
