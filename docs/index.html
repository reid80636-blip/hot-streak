Questions parents ask first.

Is FamSync really free?

Yes. FamSync is free for families of up to 4 people, with the shared calendar, chores with allowance, the grocery list and phone-free profiles. Premium makes room for 8 and adds Foxy, Remind and Reminder Calls, the morning briefing, Apple and Google Calendar sync, calendar scanning, the family map and the meal planner. It's $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a free trial for eligible new subscribers.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads in the free version or in Premium, and there won't be. Your family's data is never sold.

Does it work with the calendars we already use?

Yes, with Premium. Connect Apple Calendar and Google Calendar and events flow both ways. Each connected calendar has its own switch, and disconnecting one removes everything it brought in.

Does everyone need an iPhone?

FamSync runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Little kids and grandparents without a phone still get a profile and a color, so their chores, events and dinners show up for everyone else. FamSync isn't on Android yet.

How do reminders ring when the phone is on silent?

Remind can send a message as a banner that breaks through Focus, or as a call. Calls use the same alarm and call features iPhone keeps for things that can't be missed, so they ring from the lock screen even on silent. You choose who can call you and which events call.

Is location sharing required?

No. Location is optional and set per person. When it's on, it's only visible inside your family. Drive summaries are shown to the driver too, so a teen sees exactly what a parent sees.

Does the meal planner use AI?

No AI is needed. The planner works from what each person likes, dislikes and can't eat, and tells you why it picked each night. You can scan a recipe from a photo or paste a link, then send the missing ingredients to the grocery list.

What does Foxy do with our data?

Foxy reads your family's calendar, lists and locations to answer you, and sends the conversation to our AI provider for processing. That provider doesn't keep it. Nothing Foxy prepares is saved until you approve it. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel FamSync there. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

Who makes FamSync?

Reid, a parent and solo indie developer, under the name Pixel Drift Studios. There's no big company behind it.
